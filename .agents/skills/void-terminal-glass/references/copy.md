# Copy - Void terminal glass (v2)

Words are part of the design. Adapted from taste-skill's copy rules to a
careers product for students and early-career professionals in India, the
UAE, Europe and beyond. Write for someone reading quickly on a phone.

## Voice

Plain, specific, warm, never cute. Say what Lynx does and what the visitor
gets, with a fact where one exists (a number, a place, a date). Lynx is
"Lynx, our AI career agent" on first mention on a page, then "Lynx".

## Headlines

- **Marketing headline:** names the category and who it is for, or makes
  one plain claim. "Jobs, internships and scholarships abroad, ranked for
  you". Never a metaphor, a riddle, a slogan or a question; never "where X
  meets Y".
- **Page title (app):** where you are, as a noun phrase: "Opportunities",
  "Company boards", "What Lynx knows about you". A greeting is fine on a home
  page ("Welcome, Ada").
- **No trailing full stop** on headlines and titles (a question mark stays
  when the title is a question the visitor is asking, "Forgot your
  password?").
- **Lede:** one or two sentences, 10-40 words: how it works and the main
  benefit, or what the page is for. It is its own sentence, not the second
  half of the headline.
- **Section headings** name the thing ("Verified candidates", "Rooms and
  prices" style), 1-6 words.
- Sentence case. No all-caps, no Title Case Headings, no exclamation marks.

## Buttons and links

- A verb plus what the visitor gets, 1-4 words: Find opportunities, Create
  your account, Apply on source, Add board, Save, Run now.
- Never: Learn more, Read more, Discover, Explore, Click here, Submit, OK.
- A destructive confirmation names the thing: "Remove Acme", with "Keep" as
  the way back.
- One action keeps one wording everywhere it appears.

## Labels, hints, errors

- A field label says what goes in ("Board name", "Countries"), never a
  question; the hint shows the format or an example.
- An error says what is wrong and how to fix it, in the field's terms:
  "Use the board name from the careers-page URL: letters, numbers, dots,
  dashes." Never "Invalid input" or an error code alone.
- Success says what happened in the button's words: "Board added."
- Empty states: what is missing, then the next step. "Nothing saved yet.
  Save opportunities you like and they'll wait for you here."

## Numbers, dates, plurals

- Numbers in sentences with their unit: "1,617 opportunities", "Posted 4
  days ago", "Apply by 5 Jan 2027" (en-GB day-month order).
- Get plurals right ("1 opportunity matches", "2 opportunities match"); use
  `Intl.PluralRules` when a count is dynamic.
- Never show raw values (`NaN`, `undefined`, `0.8123`); a score shown to
  people is a label ("Strong match"), not a decimal.

## AI copy

- Lynx's reasons are one sentence, addressed to the candidate, naming the
  concrete overlap: "Your SQL and Tableau work fits this analyst role."
- Never claim what Lynx cannot know; never invent facts about a person or a
  company. AI-written text is labelled ("Lynx: ...").

## Banned words and phrases

Seamless, effortless, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, supercharge,
revolutionise, reimagine, cutting-edge, next-generation, game-changing,
powerful, robust, world-class, best-in-class, journey (for a job search),
"your one-stop shop", "where X meets Y", "take your career to the next
level", virtue words about ourselves (honest, simply, actually), and any
sentence that would fit any other company's page.
